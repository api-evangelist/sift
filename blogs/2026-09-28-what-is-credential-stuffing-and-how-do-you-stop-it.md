A credential stuffing attack doesn’t require a fraudster to guess anything. It exploits a simple reality: most people reuse passwords, and billions of those passwords are already sitting in leaked databases. Fraud and security teams who understand how this attack actually operates can catch it long before it turns into an account takeover.
