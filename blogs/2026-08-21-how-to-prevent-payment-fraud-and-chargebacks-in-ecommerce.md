Payment fraud and chargebacks used to be two separate line items on a fraud team’s dashboard. Now they are converging. Sift’s Q2 2026 Digital Trust Index found that overall chargebacks rose 19% year-over-year, and merchants lose $4.61 for every dollar of fraud once fees, fines, and lost merchandise are added up.
